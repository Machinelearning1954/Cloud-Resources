SALVAGE BRIEFING

Eight crates of botanical specimens went down with a cargo ship, scattered across the keys. Your classic floatplane 🛩 can land on the water anywhere.

Fly to the dive buoy 🚩 and touch down on the water beside it. Dive to the crate 📦 and classify the mystery iris inside — the onboard AI (a RandomForest) classifies it too.

Move: arrow keys / WASD, or tap where you want to go.
Score: +10 per correct ID, streak bonus +2/streak.
Oxygen: 45s per dive; correct IDs add +8s. Surfacing refills the tank.
