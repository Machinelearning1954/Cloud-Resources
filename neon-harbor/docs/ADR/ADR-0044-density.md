# ADR 0044 — Density Promise: Falsifiable

**Status:** RECONSTRUCTED (core contract preserved) · **Rebuilt:** 2026-11-10

## Decision

The public promise is exactly this and only this:

> **"Among games of comparable map size, Neon Harbor: Kingston ships the highest density
> of interacting systems."**

The claim is falsifiable by enumeration: count interacting systems per km² against
peer titles. What we refuse:

- "5× bigger than GTA 6" and all comparison-to-R*-numbers claims (unmeasurable, and a
  legal trap).
- Marketing numbers the build cannot demonstrate.

## Consequences

- ADR 0049's 27 amenities are a direct instrument of this promise: amenities are the
  densest measurable system layer.
- Density is counted from registry data (`data/registries/`), not from adjectives.
