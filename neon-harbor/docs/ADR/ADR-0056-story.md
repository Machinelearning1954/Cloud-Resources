# ADR 0056 — Story: "Two Tides"

**Status:** PROPOSED (story bible, first draft) · no mission rows written yet ·
**Date:** 2026-11-10 · **Directive:** ledger row 73

## Context

The user asked for "a great storyline like Power". That show is the **genre
reference** only: a prestige crime drama about a double life, loyalty and the cost of
going legitimate. No character, plot line, name, scene or dialogue is taken from it
(walls W1 and W3). The story below is original and is built from this game's own
places, venues and cast.

## Premise

**Imani Vassell** grew up between Kingston and Silver Spring. Her father, Winston
"Kingfisher" Vassell, built the most respected sound system in Kingston. When he
dies, she inherits two things: the sound system, and the truth about how he paid for
it. For thirty years the speaker crates travelling between Kingston harbour and the
Chesapeake carried contraband for a network called **the Tide Line**.

Imani wants what her father never managed: to turn the Tide Line's money and routes
into a legitimate music-and-shipping company with a flagship in Dubai. Everyone she
loves is standing on one side of that line or the other.

**The two tides** are the life she shows and the life that pays for it. The story
asks one question: *can you wash something clean without becoming what dirtied it?*

## Principal characters (all fictional)

| Character | Role | Want | Conflict with Imani |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Imani Vassell** | Protagonist; promoter, heir to the sound system | A legitimate empire her family can be proud of | Herself: every shortcut works |
| **Carver Tulloch** | Her father's partner; runs the Tide Line's street side | To keep the old business exactly as it is | Mentor first, rival by Act 2 |
| **Dane Vassell** | Her younger brother; investigator on a fictional regional port task force | To close the case that would make his career | His case is her inheritance |
| **Soraya Al-Marri** | Dubai investor and developer | A clean cultural flagship for her sea-platform quarter | She will walk the moment the money looks dirty |
| **Hiwot Tadesse** *(crew registry)* | Record producer, Imani's closest friend | To build a label on merit | She is the legitimate dream, and the most to lose |
| **Omari "Bassline" Grant** *(crew registry)* | Sound engineer who worked for Imani's father | To keep the sound system honest | He knows what the crates carried |
| **Nadine "Brass" Whyte** *(crew registry)* | Master boatwright | To keep her yard out of it | Her boats are the route |

Existing crew members keep the skilled roles they already have. None of them is
written as a criminal by default: each is pulled toward the Tide Line by loyalty,
debt or love, and each can refuse.

## Player choice and endings

Three long-running meters, shown only through how people treat Imani:

- **Clean** — how much of her business is truly legitimate.
- **Loyal** — how the crew and family trust her.
- **Heat** — how close the task force is.

Endings (Act 5):

1. **Clean Break** — she gives Dane the Tide Line and keeps the label, smaller and
   honest. Carver falls. Some of the crew never forgive her.
2. **Queen of the Tide** — she takes Carver's place. The empire is vast, the flagship
   opens, and her brother stops answering her calls.
3. **Burn the Crates** — she destroys the routes and the money both, and starts again
   with the sound system and whoever stayed.
4. **Undertow** *(failure ending)* — Heat peaks with Clean low: the task force closes
   on everyone, and the last mission is choosing who she protects.

Meters and choices are save data (codec v2). No ending is "the true one".

## How the story uses the game's systems

- **Radio (ADR 0054):** the label's releases appear on the stations as the story
  advances. The player hears their own success.
- **Property renovation (ADR 0055):** the Dubai flagship is built in stages the
  player pays for. Where the money came from changes who shows up at the opening.
- **Venues (ADR 0049):** Zinc Roof Soundhall and Riddim Ranch Studio are the story's
  home base; Keelwright Boatworks is where the two tides meet.
- **Destructible interiors (ADR 0055):** damage to the player's own venues persists
  until repaired, so violence costs something.

## Rules this story must keep

- **Ethiopia and Eritrea (ADR 0052):** Act 3's missions there are tour, studio,
  family and business scenes only. No conflict, military, border or political
  content, and no criminal plot is set in or between those two districts.
- **Washington (ADR 0052):** no government building is a venue or target. Dane's
  task force is fictional, has no real agency's name, and is shown through
  characters, not raids on real institutions (wall W4).
- **Contraband is never specified or instructed.** The story is about people, not
  methods: no real-world how-to detail for any crime.
- **Single currency** for all story rewards (ledger row 0057). No real brands,
  artists or songs appear (walls W1, W2).
- **Dialogue** in Patois and Amharic is written by native speakers (ADR 0051).
- **Mission schema (ADR 0019):** every one of the 48 rows will declare its turning
  points, checkpoints, escort and cleanup phases and gold-medal criteria. None are
  written yet.
